Who indoor cycling is for

Indoor cycling can provide a structured way to work on fitness while training indoors. Jordan's website lists indoor cycling instruction among his services.

Good coaching starts with a realistic conversation about your life outside training. Consider the time you can set aside, how much experience you have, and what would make a programme enjoyable enough to maintain. Progress is rarely about making every week perfect. A plan should give you a clear next step when life becomes busy, rather than relying on constant motivation.

When comparing coaching options, ask about how sessions are planned, what feedback you receive, how progress is reviewed and what happens if your availability changes. Those details matter more than a list of impressive-sounding promises. The right format depends on whether you prefer in-person instruction, independent training or a scheduled class.
