Frequently asked questions

How long is a session?

Training sessions are typically 55–60 minutes. Free taster appointments are 45 minutes.

Can a friend train with me?

Yes. Jordan's published information says paired sessions are available for the same session price, so you can split the cost.

Will I need a gym membership?

You will need to be a Total Fitness Wilmslow member or buy a day pass for gym-based sessions.

When do packs expire?

Jordan lists a 16-week expiry for 12-session packs and one month for packs of four. The introductory 12-pack is described as lasting three months.

Do I have to track calories?

No. Nutrition guidance can be tailored to your goals and preferences.

Who personal training is for

A face-to-face appointment is a chance to practise movement with Jordan present, ask questions as they arise and discuss the choices that make sense for your circumstances.

Good coaching starts with a realistic conversation about your life outside training. Consider the time you can set aside, how much experience you have, and what would make a programme enjoyable enough to maintain. Progress is rarely about making every week perfect. A plan should give you a clear next step when life becomes busy, rather than relying on constant motivation.

What you can expect

The published 12-session pack works out at £45 per session. A free 45-minute taster is advertised; ask about current availability and any conditions.

Before the first appointment, note what you would like to improve, the amount of time you can make available and any relevant questions for Jordan. The taster gives you a chance to see whether the approach feels right.

When comparing coaching options, ask about how sessions are planned, what feedback you receive, how progress is reviewed and what happens if your availability changes. Those details matter more than a list of impressive-sounding promises. The right format depends on whether you prefer in-person instruction, independent training or a scheduled class.

How to get started

  1. Read the published information and note the option that interests you.
  2. Use the enquiry button to ask Jordan about current availability and what the service includes.
  3. Discuss your experience, routine and any practical questions before choosing a plan.
  4. Confirm the booking details and price directly before attending or paying.

There is no need to arrive with a complete training plan. If you are unsure which service fits, a short enquiry can help clarify the differences. Jordan's in-person coaching is based at Total Fitness Wilmslow; online coaching is designed to be accessed remotely. Indoor cycling availability should be checked separately.
