Meet Jordan Crosse

Jordan offers in-person personal training in Wilmslow and online fitness coaching.

My coaching approach

Jordan coaches people who want a more balanced relationship with fitness. His approach focuses on enjoyable training, practical routines and steady progress rather than making exercise the centre of every day.

Jordan has described a sporting background that includes rugby and ultramarathons. His coaching is based at Total Fitness Wilmslow, with online coaching available for people who prefer to train independently.
