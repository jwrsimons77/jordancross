Online coaching FAQs

Do I need a gym?

Not necessarily. Your training can be designed around home equipment, although gym access may help for some strength goals.

How do check-ins work?

Jordan reviews logged workouts, feedback and progress each week; you can also send messages for extra support.

Will I need to count calories?

No. Jordan can discuss what approach works best for you.
