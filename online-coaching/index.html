Who online coaching is for

Online coaching is for people who want a plan and ongoing guidance but do not need every training session to take place alongside a trainer in Wilmslow.

Good coaching starts with a realistic conversation about your life outside training. Consider the time you can set aside, how much experience you have, and what would make a programme enjoyable enough to maintain. Progress is rarely about making every week perfect. A plan should give you a clear next step when life becomes busy, rather than relying on constant motivation.
