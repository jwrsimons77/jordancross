Questions before you join?

How do I sign up?

Review the embedded Everfit package above. If the embed does not display or checkout needs a separate window, use the direct Everfit button.

How much does online coaching cost?

Jordan’s website has listed online coaching at £60 per month. Please check the current package price and billing terms shown on Everfit before paying.

Can I train at home?

Ask Jordan whether the programme can be adapted to your available equipment and space.

Do I need to live near Wilmslow?

No. Online coaching is designed for people who want to train independently wherever they are based.

How do check-ins work?

Check the live package details on Everfit or ask Jordan about messaging and review frequency.

Can I speak to Jordan first?

Yes. Send Jordan a WhatsApp message with your goals and any questions before joining.
